It is not a law firm, a credit-repair organization, a tax preparer, or a government agency.

What this site is not

  • It is not legal advice.
  • It is not credit-repair advice and it does not promise to improve a credit score or remove any item.
  • It is not tax advice.
  • It makes no legal claims and no guarantee of any result.
  • It does not file, submit, or mail anything for you. Official links open on the companies’ and agencies’ own websites, and you decide what to send.

Verify everything with the official source

Addresses, forms, deadlines, phone numbers, and procedures can change. Before you mail a letter, freeze a file, dispute an item, or file a report, check the current instructions on the official agency or company website. If this site and the official source differ, follow the official source.

Your information stays on this device

This is a static site with no login and no server-side database. Anything you type is stored only in this browser’s localStorage unless you export a file. Do not enter a full Social Security number, a full account number, a password, or other credentials. A full backup JSON can contain the name and address you entered; keep that file private. An optional progress summary contains counts and dates only, with no names or addresses. Share that summary only if you choose to.

Disputes and identity-theft reports

A false identity-theft report is illegal. Only report or dispute items that are true and specific. An identity-theft block letter should be used only when the item is not yours and you have a genuine FTC IdentityTheft.gov report number.

About this pilot

This is a public educational pilot. Search engines are asked not to index it for now (robots.txt and a noindex tag). Those measures are a request, not a guarantee.
